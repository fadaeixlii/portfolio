# Voice & Copy Guide

> Writing rules for all copy on fadaeixlii.com.

## Voice identity
Senior consultant. Confident, specific, calm. Speaks from experience, not aspiration.

## Rules

### Tone
- First-person singular, active voice
- Past tense for case studies, present for positioning
- Short sentences. Vary rhythm. Lead with the strongest noun.
- One idea per sentence. Two sentences per paragraph on mobile.

### Specificity
- "I cut LCP from 4.2s to 1.4s by code-splitting the chart library" — YES
- "I optimized performance" — NO
- "Three years leading a four-person team" — YES
- "Extensive leadership experience" — NO

### Banned words
synergy, leverage, robust, seamless, passionate, stunning, cutting-edge,
best-in-class, innovative, revolutionary, game-changing, world-class,
empower, utilize, disrupt, delighted, thrilled, excited, amazing,
unique, dynamic, scalable (unless literally about infrastructure)

### Microcopy
- Button text: verb-first ("Send message", not "Submit")
- Error messages: name the fix, not the problem ("Enter a valid email" not "Invalid email")
- Empty states: suggest the next action ("No projects yet. Add your first one.")
- Loading: be specific ("Loading projects..." not "Please wait...")

### Headlines
- Avoid questions in headlines (they're weak)
- Lead with what the visitor gets or learns
- Numbers and specificity beat abstract claims

## Examples

### Hero tagline
- YES: "I build web applications that handle real users and real money."
- NO: "Passionate developer creating stunning digital experiences."

### About page opening
- YES: "Six years of shipping React applications to production. I've led teams, debugged memory leaks at 3am, and refactored codebases that outlived their original authors."
- NO: "I'm a full-stack developer with a passion for clean code and user-centric design."
